## 4.2 Flags

**Booking.cs has no `[Column(TypeName)]` attribute to move.** §3.5's table lists the EF-annotation item against both `PriceAlert.cs` and `Booking.cs`, but on inspection `Booking.cs` (`BookingId`, `UserId`, `FlightId`, `Flight` nav, `BookingDate`) has no price/decimal field and never had this attribute — only `PriceAlert.TargetPrice` did. This isn't a contradiction that needs a decision (there's nothing ambiguous about "there's nothing here to move"), so I proceeded with just the `PriceAlert.cs` change rather than stopping the whole item. Flagging so you're aware the brief's file list for this item was slightly over-scoped, not that anything was skipped that should've been done.

No other item turned out more involved than described, and no reference contradicted the audit report's assumptions before any deletion.
