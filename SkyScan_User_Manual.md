## 1. Introduction

SkyScan helps you search and compare live flight options across airlines, without requiring you to create an account first. If you'd like to be notified automatically when the price on a route you're watching drops, you can create a free account to unlock that feature. When you're ready to actually purchase a ticket, SkyScan redirects you to a booking partner to complete the transaction — SkyScan itself never takes payment or issues tickets.

## 2. Getting Started

You don't need to sign in to start searching. Simply open SkyScan and use the search form on the homepage. Creating an account is only required if you want to save routes and receive price-drop alerts.

## 3. Searching for Flights

1. On the homepage, choose your **trip type**: One Way, Round Trip, or Multi-City.
2. Enter your **origin city** and **destination city**. Start typing and select from the suggested list — SkyScan matches your input against known cities.
3. Choose your **departure date** (and **return date**, if you selected Round Trip).
4. If you selected **Multi-City**, add each leg of your journey (origin, destination, and date per leg).
5. Choose your **cabin class** (Economy, Premium Economy, Business, or First, depending on what's offered).
6. Click **Search**.

If SkyScan can't recognize one of the cities you typed, you'll see a message asking you to pick from the suggestion list rather than typing free text.

## 4. Understanding Your Search Results

Each result card shows:

- **Airline name** and **flight number**.
- **Departure and arrival times**, with the date shown above each time.
- **Route**, shown as origin and destination airport names with their airport codes.
- **Duration** and **number of stops** (labeled "Non-stop" if direct).
- **Price**, shown in your selected currency.
- **Amenities** available on that flight (Wi-Fi, meals, entertainment), shown when you open the flight details.

If you searched Round Trip, each card shows both your **Outbound Leg** and **Return Leg** stacked in the same card, since they're priced and booked together as one combined fare.

You can narrow results using the **price range slider** and the **airline filter**, both of which adjust automatically to the actual range of prices and airlines found in your results.

## 5. Viewing Flight Details

Click **Flight Details** on any result to open a detailed view showing the full itinerary (including the return leg, if applicable), duration, stops, and amenity icons (Wi-Fi, meals, power/entertainment) for that specific flight.

## 6. Proceeding to Book

When you're ready to purchase:

1. Click the **booking/continue** action on the flight or flight-details view you want.
2. SkyScan records that you're interested in this flight (see Section 7/9 depending on whether you're signed in) and then **redirects you to an external site** to complete your purchase.
3. Complete the actual booking and payment on that external site — SkyScan does not process payment and cannot complete the purchase on your behalf.

**Important:** the price and exact flight shown on the external site may not always match exactly what you saw on SkyScan, since the destination site may draw from a different inventory system. Always confirm the price and flight details on the external site before paying. See Section 15 for more on this.

## 7. Guest "My Bookings" (No Account Needed)

If you're not signed in, SkyScan still remembers the flights you've proceeded to book, using a secure cookie stored in your browser — no account required. Visit **My Bookings** to see this list. Flights whose departure date has already passed are automatically removed from the list. Because this list lives in a cookie, it's tied to your current browser only — using a different browser or device, or clearing your cookies, will not carry the list over.

## 8. Creating an Account

You can create an account if you want to save routes and get price-drop alerts (see Section 9).

- **Sign up with email:** provide your name, email, and password. You'll need to confirm your email address before some account features are available.
- **Sign up with Google:** click **Continue with Google** and sign in with your existing Google account — no separate password to remember.

## 9. Favoriting a Route & Price Alerts (Registered Users)

This feature requires being signed in.

1. On any search result, click the **Favorite** button (heart icon).
2. SkyScan saves this as a watched route with your current price as the target.
3. Approximately every 6 hours, SkyScan automatically re-checks the route in the background.
4. If the same flight — or a cheaper alternative on the same route — drops below your saved target price, you'll receive an **email notification** with the details, including any cheaper alternatives found.
5. Once an alert successfully notifies you, it's automatically removed — favorite the route again if you'd like to keep watching it afterward.

Clicking **Favorite** again on the same route before it fires removes the alert (un-favorites it).

## 10. Your Profile

Your profile page shows the routes/flights you currently have price alerts on, including the route, dates, and target price for each. This is also where you manage your account security settings (see Section 11).

## 11. Managing Your Account Security

- **Change password:** available from your profile if you registered with email/password.
- **Two-factor authentication:** you can enable two-factor authentication for an extra layer of login security, and disable it later if you no longer want it.
- **Email confirmation:** if you registered with email, some actions may require confirming your email address first — check your inbox for a confirmation link after signing up.

## 12. Trending Routes

The homepage displays a set of currently popular routes, based on recent search activity from all SkyScan users. This is a discovery aid for travelers who haven't decided where to go yet — click a trending route to jump straight into a search for it.

## 13. Currency Display

Prices are shown converted to your selected currency. If you'd like to see prices in a different currency, use the currency selector; SkyScan converts using current exchange rates.

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I book my flight directly on SkyScan?**
No. SkyScan finds and compares flights, but you complete the actual purchase on the partner site you're redirected to.

**Why did the price change when I got redirected?**
SkyScan's search results and the partner site's checkout are separate systems with separate pricing and inventory. SkyScan shows you the best information available at search time, but the final price is always set by whichever site you complete your purchase on. Always confirm the price there before paying.

**Do I need an account to search?**
No — searching, viewing results, viewing flight details, and being redirected to book are all available without an account.

**Do I need an account to get price-drop alerts?**
Yes — favoriting a route and receiving automated email alerts requires a registered account, since alerts are tied to your email address.

**What happens to my guest booking list if I create an account later?**
Guest bookings are stored in a browser cookie and are not automatically transferred to a new account — they're separate mechanisms.

**How often are price alerts checked?**
Approximately every 6 hours.

## 15. Important Disclaimers

- SkyScan is a **search and comparison tool**, not a travel agency or booking platform. It does not process payments, issue tickets, or confirm reservations.
- Prices shown on SkyScan are sourced from a flight-data provider and are **indicative** — always confirm the final price and flight details on the partner site before completing a purchase.
- Because SkyScan and its redirect destinations are separate systems, an exact match between what you see on SkyScan and what you see at checkout elsewhere **cannot be guaranteed**.
- Price alert emails are based on periodic automated checks (roughly every 6 hours), not real-time monitoring — a fare could rise or fall again between checks.
