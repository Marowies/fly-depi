## Brand & Style
The design system embodies a "Quiet Luxury" aesthetic—sophisticated, exclusive, and intentionally restrained. It targets a high-end audience that values craftsmanship over clutter. 

The style is a fusion of **Modern Minimalism** and **High-Contrast Editorial**. It utilizes expansive whitespace, razor-sharp grid execution, and a dramatic typographic scale to evoke a sense of prestige. Every element is designed to feel curated, using subtle transitions and high-quality finishes rather than aggressive visual effects.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in a "Champagne and Charcoal" spectrum, now optimized for a refined Light Mode experience.

- **Primary Gold (#D8B46A):** Used sparingly for key calls to action, active states, or brand accents.
- **Deep Bronze (#8A5A2B):** Used for borders, subtle icons, or secondary button states to provide warmth.
- **Light Mode (Default):** Shifts to a "Bone White" or "Gallery Grey" environment to maintain a soft, curated feel that emphasizes clarity and high-end photography.
- **Dark Mode:** Utilizes "Infinite Black" (#111111) for backgrounds to make gold accents and high-contrast elements pop.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy relies on the tension between the expressive, high-contrast serifs of **Playfair Display** and the functional, contemporary precision of **Hanken Grotesk**.

- **Headlines:** Should be set with tight line-spacing and slight negative letter-spacing for a "masthead" look. 
- **Body Text:** Uses Hanken Grotesk for maximum legibility. Line height is generous (1.5x - 1.6x) to create a breathable, relaxed reading experience.
- **Labels:** Always uppercase with wide tracking (10%+) to denote technical information or navigational headers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout philosophy is a **Fixed Central Grid** for desktop and a **Fluid Margin Grid** for mobile. 

- **Generous Margins:** Desktop layouts use an 80px outer margin to frame content like a luxury magazine.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** A strict 8px base unit is used. Section headers should have significant vertical breathing room (120px+) to distinguish different content pillars.
- **Alignment:** Content is primarily left-aligned for readability, but "Display" moments can be centered for dramatic effect.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system avoids heavy shadows, favoring **Tonal Layers** and **Low-Contrast Outlines**.

- **Surfaces:** In Light Mode, depth is created by shifting background colors (e.g., from a pure bone white to a slightly warmer grey).
- **Borders:** Instead of shadows, use 1px solid borders in Bronze (#8A5A2B) at 20-30% opacity to define cards and containers.
- **Glassmorphism:** Use sparingly for navigation overlays. A subtle backdrop blur (12px) with a light tint provides a sophisticated transition between layers.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Primary buttons are solid gold (#D8B46A) with dark text. Secondary buttons use a 1px bronze border with no fill. All buttons are rectangular and use the `label-sm` typographic style.
- **Inputs:** Minimalist bottom-border only or a very thin 1px full border. Focus states transition the border color to gold.
- **Cards:** No shadows. Cards are defined by a background color slightly different than the page background or a subtle 1px border.
- **Lists:** Separated by thin, full-width rules (0.5px) in bronze or light grey.
- **Imagery:** Large, full-bleed images or images with generous white-space padding. Use "Reveal" animations where the image slides up into place.
- **Navigation:** Top-tier navigation uses wide letter spacing and no icons, emphasizing the typographic elegance.
